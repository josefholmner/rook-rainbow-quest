# World 2, level 19: The Royal Puzzle

Version: 0.10.0. Stable ID: `mate-19`.

## Teaching goal

Decide which defender to remove before giving check.

## Board and pieces

6 × 6. Player: queen b1, rook a1, rook c1, bishop b3, knight b2, pawn a2, pawn d2. Opponent: king f6, pawn e5, pawn d5, rook c5, pawn b5, bishop f4.

A bishop on b3 points across the board, supported by a d2 pawn.

## Rules and tuning

Player moves first, with no king. Opponent has exactly one king. Checkmate wins; its king cannot be captured. Every enemy move must leave the king safe, including during periodic capture oversights and brave play. Stalemate offers a free retry. Pawns become queens on the far rank. No undo, move limit, time limit, lives, or full game over.

Opponent: overlook every 6 replies, safety 3, brave after 4 quiet rounds. Change positions and policy in the paired JSON, then regenerate and test its winning route. The icon shows queen against a crowned king.

## Tested route

2 player moves against the configured deterministic opponent: c1 → c5, c5 → c6. The enemy replies after every non-winning move. This is a validated winning route, not necessarily the shortest route. Remaining enemy helpers do not need to be captured.

Regenerate with `cargo run --release --example author_matches -- --world2`, update this description, then run the full test suite.
