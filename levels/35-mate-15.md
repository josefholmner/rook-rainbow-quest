# World 2, level 15: The Royal Rook

Version: 0.10.0. Stable ID: `mate-15`.

## Teaching goal

The enemy rook fights back. Trade when it helps.

## Board and pieces

6 × 6. Player: queen b1, rook a1, rook c1, bishop d1, knight b2, pawn b3, pawn c2. Opponent: king f6, pawn e5, pawn d5, rook c5, pawn b5.

The advanced b3 pawn supports the first encounter with the royal rook.

## Rules and tuning

Player moves first, with no king. Opponent has exactly one king. Checkmate wins; its king cannot be captured. Every enemy move must leave the king safe, including during periodic capture oversights and brave play. Stalemate offers a free retry. Pawns become queens on the far rank. No undo, move limit, time limit, lives, or full game over.

Opponent: overlook every 5 replies, safety 2, brave after 4 quiet rounds. Change positions and policy in the paired JSON, then regenerate and test its winning route. The icon shows queen against a crowned king.

## Tested route

4 player moves against the configured deterministic opponent: a1 → a5, a5 → c5, b1 → a1, a1 → a6. The enemy replies after every non-winning move. This is a validated winning route, not necessarily the shortest route. Remaining enemy helpers do not need to be captured.

Regenerate with `cargo run --release --example author_matches -- --world2`, update this description, then run the full test suite.
