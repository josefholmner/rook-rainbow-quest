# World 2, level 2: Two Rooks Take Turns

Version: 0.8.2. Stable ID: `mate-02`.

## Teaching goal

The queen helps the rooks cover escape squares, giving the child another gentle checkmate exercise before enemy helpers join in level 3. Ladder mate knowledge is not required.

## Board and pieces

6 × 6. Player: rook a1, rook b2, queen f1. Opponent: king d4.

## Rules and tuning

Player moves first, with no king. Opponent has exactly one king. Checkmate wins; its king cannot be captured. Every enemy move must leave the king safe, including during periodic capture oversights and brave play. Stalemate offers a free retry. Pawns become queens on the far rank. No undo, move limit, time limit, lives, or full game over.

Opponent: overlook every 3 replies, safety 0, brave after 2 quiet rounds. Change positions and policy in the paired JSON, then regenerate and test its winning route. The icon shows rook against a crowned king.

## Tested route

3 player moves against the configured deterministic opponent: a1 → c1, b2 → b3, b3 → d3. The queen covers escape squares while the rooks give check. The enemy replies after every non-winning move. This is a validated winning route, not necessarily the shortest route. Remaining enemy helpers do not need to be captured.

Regenerate with `cargo run --release --example author_matches -- --world2`, update this description, then run the full test suite.
