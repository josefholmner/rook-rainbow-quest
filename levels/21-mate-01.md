# World 2, level 1: Hello, King!

Version: 0.8.2. Stable ID: `mate-01`.

## Teaching goal

Use the queen to help the rooks cover the king's escapes. The extra queen makes this first checkmate lesson forgiving without requiring the child to know ladder mate.

## Board and pieces

6 × 6. Player: rook a1, rook b3, queen f1. Opponent: king d4.

## Rules and tuning

Player moves first, with no king. Opponent has exactly one king. Checkmate wins; its king cannot be captured. Every enemy move must leave the king safe, including during periodic capture oversights and brave play. Stalemate offers a free retry. Pawns become queens on the far rank. No undo, move limit, time limit, lives, or full game over.

Opponent: overlook every 3 replies, safety 0, brave after 2 quiet rounds. Change positions and policy in the paired JSON, then regenerate and test its winning route. The icon shows rook against a crowned king.

## Tested route

2 player moves against the configured deterministic opponent: a1 → d1, f1 → b5. The enemy replies after every non-winning move. This is a validated winning route, not necessarily the shortest route. Remaining enemy helpers do not need to be captured.

Regenerate with `cargo run --release --example author_matches -- --world2`, update this description, then run the full test suite.
