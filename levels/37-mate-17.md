# World 2, level 17: Clear the Diagonal

Version: 0.7.2. Stable ID: `mate-17`.

## Teaching goal

Clear a diagonal, then cover the king's last escape.

## Board and pieces

6 × 6. Player: queen b1, rook a1, rook c1, bishop d1, knight a3, pawn a2, pawn c2. Opponent: king f6, pawn e5, pawn d5, rook c5, pawn b5.

## Rules and tuning

Player moves first, with no king. Opponent has exactly one king. Checkmate wins; its king cannot be captured. Every enemy move must leave the king safe, including during periodic capture oversights and brave play. Stalemate offers a free retry. Pawns become queens on the far rank. No undo, move limit, time limit, lives, or full game over.

Opponent: overlook every 6 replies, safety 2, brave after 4 quiet rounds. Change positions and policy in the paired JSON, then regenerate and test its winning route. The icon shows queen against a crowned king.

## Tested route

4 player moves against the configured deterministic opponent: b1 → b4, d1 → f3, f3 → d5, c1 → f1. The enemy replies after every non-winning move. This is a validated winning route, not necessarily the shortest route. Remaining enemy helpers do not need to be captured.

Regenerate with `cargo run --release --example author_matches -- --world2`, update this description, then run the full test suite.
