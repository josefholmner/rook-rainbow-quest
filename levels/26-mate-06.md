# World 2, level 6: A Knight Visits

Version: 0.10.0. Stable ID: `mate-06`.

## Teaching goal

Watch the knight while you close the king's escape routes.

## Board and pieces

6 × 6. Player: queen c1, rook a1, rook b2, pawn e2, pawn f2. Opponent: king d4, pawn c3, knight a4.

Two friendly pawns join the rooks against the visiting knight.

## Rules and tuning

Player moves first, with no king. Opponent has exactly one king. Checkmate wins; its king cannot be captured. Every enemy move must leave the king safe, including during periodic capture oversights and brave play. Stalemate offers a free retry. Pawns become queens on the far rank. No undo, move limit, time limit, lives, or full game over.

Opponent: overlook every 4 replies, safety 0, brave after 2 quiet rounds. Change positions and policy in the paired JSON, then regenerate and test its winning route. The icon shows queen against a crowned king.

## Tested route

3 player moves against the configured deterministic opponent: b2 → b5, a1 → a4, c1 → e1. The enemy replies after every non-winning move. This is a validated winning route, not necessarily the shortest route. Remaining enemy helpers do not need to be captured.

Regenerate with `cargo run --release --example author_matches -- --world2`, update this description, then run the full test suite.
